# FlowApp — Product Brief and Backstory

## Overview

FlowApp is a fictional B2B/B2C Productivity and Work Management SaaS Platform used as the demonstration use case for the Feedback Intelligence Agent.

Think of it as a direct competitor to tools like Asana, Monday.com, Notion, or Trello. It is designed to help individuals and teams organise their work — complex enough to generate the wide variety of user feedback the Feedback Intelligence Agent is built to analyse.

---

## The Elevator Pitch

"FlowApp is a cross-platform work management app that combines task tracking, calendar sync, and team collaboration. It serves everyone from solo freelancers to large enterprise teams looking to streamline their daily operations."

---

## Core Features

### Task Management
Kanban boards, customisable list views, and due-date pickers. The most-used feature across all user segments.

### Time Tracking and Reporting
Analytics dashboards and time-tracking features to measure team efficiency. Primary pain point for enterprise users who need detailed reporting for billing and client management.

### Cross-Platform Sync
Available on Web, Mac, iOS, and Android. Includes offline mode for mobile field teams. Sync reliability is the most common source of negative App Store reviews.

### Enterprise Administration
SSO (Single Sign-On), role-based permission settings, and API access for custom workflows. Primary selling point for mid-market and enterprise accounts. SSO failures are a common escalation trigger in sales calls.

### Export and Integrations
PDF exports and calendar integrations. Export functionality is a recurring pain point — frequently mentioned as a feature request disguised as a complaint in user feedback across all three sources.

## Business Model

### B2C — Consumer and Freelancer Tier

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|---------|
| Free | $0/month | Up to 3 projects, basic task management |
| Pro | $10-30/month (~$120-360/year) | Unlimited projects, time tracking, calendar sync |

Free and Pro users typically leave feedback via App Store reviews and NPS surveys. Their feedback focuses on UX, performance, and missing features they expect from competitors like Notion or Trello.

### B2B — Mid-Market and Enterprise Tier

| Plan | ARR Range | Features |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Team | $2,000-$5,000 | Up to 50 seats, basic SSO, reporting |
| Enterprise | $5,000-$15,000 | Unlimited seats, full SSO, API access, dedicated support |

Enterprise users typically appear in sales call notes and NPS surveys. Their feedback focuses on SSO reliability, API limitations, reporting depth, and export capabilities. Churn risk is highest in this segment because switching costs are lower than expected and competitors actively target them.

## Mock Data Sources

### App Store Reviews — 50 items

Mobile users reviewing FlowApp on iOS and Android. Mix of free, pro, and enterprise users.

Feedback types included:
- Genuine positive reviews
- Direct bug reports
- Sarcastic negative reviews (positive words, low rating)
- Feature requests disguised as complaints
- Mixed sentiment reviews
- Escalation signals (competitor mentions, churn intent)
- Vague or neutral reviews

Common topics: sync reliability, export functionality, offline mode, performance on older devices, onboarding experience.

---

### NPS Survey Responses — 30 items

Surveys triggered after key user actions:
- Post-onboarding (new user first week)
- Post-export-attempt (after using export)
- Monthly check-in (existing users)

Feedback types included:
- Promoters (score 9-10) with positive comments
- Passives (score 7-8) with neutral comments
- Detractors (score 0-6) with negative comments
- Detractors with hidden feature requests

Common topics: time tracking accuracy, calendar sync reliability, reporting depth, SSO setup    complexity, mobile performance.

---

### Sales Call Notes — 20 items

Notes from enterprise sales conversations:
- Quarterly business reviews
- Renewal discussions
- Support escalation calls
- Competitive evaluation calls

Feedback types included:
- Healthy accounts with positive notes
- Accounts with specific product complaints
- Accounts with churn risk
- Accounts actively evaluating competitors

Common topics: SSO reliability, API rate limits, reporting for client billing, export to Excel, mobile offline mode for field teams, onboarding support quality.

ARR range: $2,000 to $15,000 per account.
